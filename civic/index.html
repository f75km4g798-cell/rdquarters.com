What is a license plate reader?

An automated license plate reader (ALPR) is a camera system that can record a vehicle's license plate and associated information such as time, location and images. Depending on the system and its settings, those records can be searched and shared. This raises real public-safety opportunities and real questions about limits and oversight.

01 · THE DEVICE

One camera sees a car.

It can record the plate and where and when it was observed. It is not automatically proof of wrongdoing.

A public checklist

Questions every resident can ask

What problem?

What specific local problem is the system meant to solve, and what evidence shows it can help?

What limits?

How long are records kept? Who can search or share them? Are searches audited?

What accountability?

What does it cost? How is success measured? What happens when rules are broken?
