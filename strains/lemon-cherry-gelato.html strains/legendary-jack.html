RD Quarters Grade System

Batch Quality Is Separate from Strain Identity

A strain profile records the cultivar name and its known or reported characteristics. The grade records the quality position of a particular batch at a particular time.

Legendary Jack could therefore appear as Classic now and later return as Select or Reserve if a different grower or batch provides stronger aroma, structure, freshness, cure and verified information.
